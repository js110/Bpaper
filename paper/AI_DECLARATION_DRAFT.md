# Declaration of generative AI and AI-assisted technologies — author-approval draft

Elsevier currently requires a separate disclosure immediately before the references when generative AI was used substantively in manuscript preparation. This file is a factual draft only and must be approved by all responsible authors before it is inserted into the manuscript.

## Proposed statement

During the preparation of this work, the authors used OpenAI ChatGPT to support research organization, software implementation, numerical-analysis checking, manuscript drafting and editing, and the preparation of two conceptual schematics. The two schematics were generated with OpenAI image-generation functionality via ChatGPT and were subsequently checked against the stated system model and analytical calculation. Quantitative experimental figures and tables were generated from archived executable code and stored simulation outputs rather than from generative image tools. The authors must review and edit all AI-assisted material as needed and, upon approving the final manuscript, take full responsibility for the content of the publication.

## Required author action before submission

All authors should confirm that the statement accurately describes the tools and purposes, and that they accept responsibility for the final manuscript. After that confirmation, insert the approved statement immediately before the references under the heading required by Elsevier.
